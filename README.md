# Sports News and Video Streaming App

Task 5.1C

In this task I have successfully completed both the subtask. 
# 1. Sports News Feed App
  In this app, I have made only a single activity and added both the horizontal and vertical recycler view.
  I have made an amazing UI/UX, where i have added the navigation between the two apps on the top, horizontal recycler view for all the latest news, and vertixal recycler view for all the news.
  I have also given tags in each news for the filtering option, the title and desciption for the news section are hardcoded, and used free images for  demonstrating the visual images and also i have added bookmark feature such that they can see all the news articles which they have added for viewing later.
  The news articles are designed in such a way that when we open a news section then on scrolling we can see the related articles of the same category as well. 
  
# 2. iStream - Personal Video Playlist App
I have followed the exact wireframes provided in the task and designed the login,sign-up, and playlist page, the user activity is simple, they just have to signup initialy and then login and paste their youtube link to play, or they can add it to their playlist as well. 
They also have options to add videos or remove the video from the playlist. 
On the top right, the user also have the option to logout from the app.
